# Minimality certificates

Goal: make lower bounds from the retained abstract-profile axioms independently checkable as exact nonnegative linear combinations. This track continues the least-profile theorem in root `proof.tex`, introduced in merged PR #2.

Open questions: find smaller certificates, characterize which raw axioms are indispensable for individual targets, and extend certificates to genuinely additional constraints. A certificate here does not verify tensor realizability or upstream entropy inequalities. There were no other research tracks at this contribution's base commit.
